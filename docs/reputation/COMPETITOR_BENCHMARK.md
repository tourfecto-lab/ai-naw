## What this says
- Where prices are published they are **per location / per domain**, and high: $80 to $150 per location per month (Reputation.com), $319+ per domain per month (Trustpilot, annual billing).
- Review-invitation volume is the main usage limit (Trustpilot: 50 / 300 / 1000 per month); replies are unlimited or not metered on the pricing pages.
- Birdeye and Podium hide prices behind sales, so there is no verified market anchor for them.
- Tourfecto includes reputation inside the platform plans (80 / 300 / 1000 AI replies per month on Starter / Growth / Leadership, plus a 200-reply pack for $8). A stand-alone reputation plan only makes sense for customers who want reputation without the rest of the platform.
